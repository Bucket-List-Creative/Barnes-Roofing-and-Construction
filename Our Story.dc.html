Our story

Small on purpose.

Barnes Roofing & Construction is a small, family-owned company in Cottleville, Missouri, built so every home gets real attention from the first conversation to the final inspection.

Where it started

A decade in the industry

I started in the roofing industry more than a decade ago with a small company. Over the years, I helped it grow as general manager into a business doing more than $20 million in work. We had a great team of people who cared about what they did.

But as the company grew, I saw how having so many people involved in each project could make it harder to deliver the consistent quality and personal attention every homeowner deserves.

Why Barnes

Personally involved in every project

That experience shaped why I started Barnes Roofing & Construction. I wanted to build a small, family-owned company where I could stay personally involved in every project, from the first conversation through the final inspection.

That allows me to pay attention to the details, stand behind our work, and make sure every customer receives exceptional service.
